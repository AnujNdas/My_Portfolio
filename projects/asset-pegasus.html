AssetPegasus.

A web-based asset management platform for organizing hardware and software inventory, assignments, and subscription workflows.

2026Live project
OVERVIEW

The idea
behind the build.

AssetPegasus is an asset management SaaS project focused on helping organizations manage hardware and software assets through a centralized application. The platform includes tenant-aware access, inventory workflows, asset assignments, and subscription management.

The product is built around organization-level data separation and role-based workflows, with a focus on making asset administration more structured and accessible.
